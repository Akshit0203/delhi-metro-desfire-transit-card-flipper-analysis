# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the integrity of the research.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Using the project to facilitate illegal activity, fraud, or unauthorized access.
- Sharing real cryptographic keys, private card data, or other sensitive material.
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and in public spaces when an individual
is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
project maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a
response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.
